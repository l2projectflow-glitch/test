Complete NPC buffer

  • NPC buffer in all towns, complete, with ready-made schemes, and each player can also create 6 custom buff schemes with a name and 24 buff slots. All buffs last 1 hour and 30 minutes, and for VIP characters the buffs last 3 hours.
  • [AIO] characters provide 3-hour buffs, but they have no advantage over the NPC buffs besides the duration. As mentioned above, only VIP characters have 3-hour buffs, so the AIO is simply a way to get longer buffs without having a VIP character.
